## 2. Why Do Prayer Times Differ Between Apps?
Labels: Prayer Times, Namaz

Two apps, same city, different Fajr time? Usually both are right, they just use different settings:

1. **Fajr/Isha angle:** Karachi uses 18°/18°, Muslim World League 18°/17°, ISNA 15°/15°, and Umm al-Qura puts Isha 90 minutes after Maghrib.
2. **Asr:** Hanafi Asr starts when an object's shadow is twice its length, Shafi'i when it equals its length, so Hanafi Asr is later.

The safest choice is the timetable your local masjid follows. Islam 365 picks the usual method for your country automatically, and you can change it in Settings.

## 3. Finding the Qibla on Your Phone: 4 Quick Tips
Labels: Qibla, Travel

- Hold the phone **flat**, away from metal, magnets and speakers.
- **Calibrate** by moving the phone in a figure-of-eight a few times.
- Make sure the app knows your **current city** after travelling.
- From Pakistan and India the Qibla is roughly west, slightly south of west. From North America it is to the **north-east**, because the Earth is round.

Islam 365 has a Qibla compass with the distance to Makkah. Step-by-step guide: https://islam365-app.vercel.app/blog/how-to-find-qibla-direction-on-your-phone/
