Umrah takes only a few hours once you reach Makkah. The four steps in one minute:

  1. Ihram before the miqat: bath, two white sheets for men, intention, and the talbiyah Labbayk Allahumma labbayk...
  2. Tawaf: seven rounds around the Kaaba starting at the Black Stone, then two rakat behind Maqam Ibrahim and Zamzam.
  3. Sa'i: seven rounds between Safa and Marwa, starting at Safa and ending at Marwa.
  4. Halq or taqsir: men shave or shorten the hair, women cut a fingertip's length.

"One Umrah to the next is an expiation for what is between them" (Bukhari, Muslim).
