Looking for work or worried about money? Say the dua Musa (AS) made when he had nothing in a strange land:

Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqir.
My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send down to me. (Quran 28:24)

And after Fajr, the Prophet ﷺ would ask: Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbala: beneficial knowledge, pure provision and accepted deeds (Ibn Majah).
