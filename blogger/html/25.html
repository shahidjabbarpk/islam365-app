Flying across a prayer time? Five quick rules scholars give:

  1. If you can, pray before boarding or after landing (travellers shorten four-rakat prayers).
  2. In the air, use the time where the plane actually is: look at the sun out of the window.
  3. Make wudu with little water in the toilet; if impossible, tayammum.
  4. Face the Qibla at least at the start if you can; ask the crew for the direction.
  5. Stand if there is space; if not, pray seated and bow your head for ruku and sujood.
