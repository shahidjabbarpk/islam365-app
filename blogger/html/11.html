Feeling anxious? The Prophet ﷺ often said this dua (Sahih Bukhari):

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan, wal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-bukhli wal-jubn, wa dala'id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijal.

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, weakness and laziness, miserliness and cowardice, the burden of debt and being overpowered by people.

And the dua of Yunus (AS) from the belly of the whale: La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin (Quran 21:87).

Alongside dua: guard your salah, keep your morning and evening azkar, and talk to someone you trust. Getting help from a doctor or counsellor does not contradict tawakkul.
