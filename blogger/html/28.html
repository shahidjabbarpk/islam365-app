Exam tomorrow? Two Quranic duas every student should know:

  • Rabbi zidni 'ilma: My Lord, increase me in knowledge (20:114).
  • Rabbish-rahli sadri, wa yassir li amri, wahlul 'uqdatam-min lisani, yafqahu qawli: My Lord, expand my chest, ease my task and untie my tongue so they understand me (20:25-28).

Plus: Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla...: nothing is easy except what You make easy (Ibn Hibban).
