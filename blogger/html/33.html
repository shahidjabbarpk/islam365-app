Six authentic adhkar for the morning (after Fajr) and evening (after Asr), about ten minutes in total:

  1. Ayatul Kursi, once
  2. Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas, three times each
  3. Sayyidul Istighfar, once
  4. Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un..., three times
  5. Raditu billahi Rabba, wa bil-Islami dina, wa bi Muhammadin nabiyya, three times
  6. SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi, 100 times
