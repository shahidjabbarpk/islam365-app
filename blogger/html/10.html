The dua the Prophet ﷺ made most often, according to Anas (RA) in Sahih Bukhari, is only one line long:

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. (Quran 2:201)

Three more short Rabbana duas to learn this week:

  • Rabbana taqabbal minna innaka antas-sami'ul-'alim: Our Lord, accept from us (2:127)
  • Rabbana zalamna anfusana...: the dua of Adam (AS) for forgiveness (7:23)
  • Rabbanagh-fir li wa li walidayya...: for yourself, your parents and all believers (14:41)
