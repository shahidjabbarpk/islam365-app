Istikhara is a two-rakat prayer followed by a special dua, asking Allah to choose what is best for you. The Prophet ﷺ taught it to his companions "just as he taught them a surah of the Quran" (Sahih Bukhari).

Three things people often get wrong:

  1. You don't need a dream. After Istikhara, go ahead with what seems right; if Allah makes it easy, that is your answer.
  2. Pray it yourself. There is no need to pay anyone to do Istikhara for you.
  3. Also ask for advice from wise, trustworthy people. Istikhara and mashwara go together.
