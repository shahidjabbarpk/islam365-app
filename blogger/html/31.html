Missing prayers because notifications arrive late? On Android 12+, three settings fix it:

  1. Allow Alarms & reminders for your prayer app (otherwise Android can delay notifications up to an hour).
  2. Settings → Apps → your prayer app → Battery → Unrestricted.
  3. On Xiaomi turn on Autostart; on Samsung remove it from Sleeping apps; on Oppo/Vivo allow background activity.

Full 6-step setup: https://islam365-app.vercel.app/blog/azan-notification-android-phone/
