New to salah, or teaching your kids? The whole prayer comes down to a few positions:

  1. Takbir: Allahu Akbar, hands raised to the ears.
  2. Qiyam: Thana, Surah Al-Fatiha and a short surah.
  3. Ruku: Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim three times.
  4. Sujood twice: Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la three times.
  5. Tashahhud: At-tahiyyat, Durood Ibrahimi and a dua.
  6. Salam to the right and left.

Fajr has 2 fard, Dhuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3 and Isha 4.
