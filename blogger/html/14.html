Dua can be made any time, but authentic hadith point to special moments when it is most likely to be answered. Here are four you can catch every single day:

  1. Between the azan and iqamah: "Dua between the azan and the iqamah is not rejected" (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi).
  2. In sujood: "The closest a servant is to his Lord is in prostration, so make plenty of dua" (Sahih Muslim).
  3. The last third of the night: when Allah asks "Who is calling upon Me that I may answer him?" (Bukhari, Muslim).
  4. For a brother in his absence: an angel says "Ameen, and for you the same" (Sahih Muslim).
