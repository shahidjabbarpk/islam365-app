Hajj in one glance (Tamattu'):

  • 8th Dhul Hijjah: ihram for Hajj, go to Mina.
  • 9th: Arafah until sunset ("Hajj is Arafah", Tirmidhi), then Muzdalifah for the night.
  • 10th: stone the big Jamarah, sacrifice, shave or shorten, Tawaf al-Ifadah and sa'i.
  • 11th-12th (13th): stone all three Jamarat each day in Mina.
  • Before leaving: farewell tawaf.
