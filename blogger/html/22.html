Memorising the Quran as an adult is about consistency, not talent. Three tips that make the biggest difference:

  1. Listen before you read. Play each verse 5-10 times from a clear reciter first.
  2. Revise more than you learn. The Quran "escapes faster than a camel from its rope" (Sahih Bukhari): revise yesterday's portion and one older portion every day.
  3. Use it in salah. Recite your new surah in sunnah and nafl prayers.
