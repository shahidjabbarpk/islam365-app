The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best fasting after Ramadan is in Allah's month of Muharram" (Sahih Muslim).

Fasting Ashura (10 Muharram) expiates the sins of the previous year (Sahih Muslim), and it is sunnah to fast the 9th as well.
