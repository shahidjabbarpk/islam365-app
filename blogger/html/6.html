Tahajjud is best prayed in the last third of the night, when Allah descends to the lowest heaven and asks: "Who is calling upon Me that I may answer him?" (Bukhari, Muslim).

Quick way to find it: take tonight's Maghrib and tomorrow's Fajr time, find the gap, divide by three, and subtract that from Fajr. Example: Maghrib 5:15 pm, Fajr 5:00 am, so the night is 11 h 45 min, a third is about 3 h 55 min, and the last third starts around 1:05 am.

  • Pray two rakat at a time; even two rakat is Tahajjud.
  • End with Witr if you have not prayed it after Isha.
  • Start with one night a week and build up.
