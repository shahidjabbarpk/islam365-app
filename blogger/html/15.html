Five tiny duas a child can learn this month, one for each part of the day:

  • Before eating: Bismillah
  • After eating: Alhamdulillah
  • Leaving home: Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'alallah
  • Before sleeping: Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya (Sahih Bukhari)
  • For learning: Rabbi zidni 'ilma, "My Lord, increase me in knowledge" (Quran 20:114)

Best tip: say them aloud together every time. Children copy what we do far more than what we tell them.
