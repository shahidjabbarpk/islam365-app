Meaning of As-Saboor

As-Saboor (الصَّبُورُ) means The Most Patient. In Urdu: بڑا صبر کرنے والا. Allah says: "And to Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them" (Quran 7:180).

Calling upon Allah with this name

The Prophet ﷺ taught us to call Allah by the name that matches our need. You can begin your dua with Ya Saboor, then ask for what you need, with conviction and humility.

Why learn the 99 names?

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names; whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise" (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim). Scholars explain this as learning them, understanding them and living by their meaning. Read our guide: how to learn the 99 names in a month · اسماء الحسنیٰ اردو.
