Why Pakistan's date can be one day different

The date above follows the Umm al-Qura calendar used in Saudi Arabia. In Pakistan, India and Bangladesh each month starts when the new moon is sighted locally, which is often one day later. So if today is the 15th here, it may be the 14th in Pakistan. For Ramadan and Eid, always follow your local moon-sighting committee (Ruet-e-Hilal in Pakistan).
