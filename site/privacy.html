Islam 365 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Islam 365 (package com.islam365.mza) is a free app for tasbeeh counting, prayer times and reminders, Qibla direction, Quran with translations and recitation, duas, hadith, a prayer tracker, a zakat calculator and the Hijri calendar. We respect your privacy: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Your name

The app has no accounts, logins or passwords. On first start it asks for your name, which is saved only on your phone and used only to greet you inside the app and in its reminders. It is never sent to us or to any server.

Changing or deleting your data

You can change your name at any time in Settings → Your name. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data in your phone settings) removes your name and all app data. Because we never receive any of it, there is nothing stored with us to delete. If you need help, write to the contact address below.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. When you open the app it may refresh this location so prayer times follow you when you travel. The location is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us. To show your city's name, the phone's built-in location service (for example Google Play services on Android) may be asked to look up the coordinates. You can instead choose a city manually, and you can turn off the permission at any time in your phone settings.

Notifications

Prayer-time reminders, the daily ayah/hadith/dua reminder and the Friday reminder are all scheduled locally on your phone. No notification data leaves your device. You can turn each one off in Settings. The app asks for permission to set exact alarms only so that reminders arrive on time.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to download public content: hadith chapters (cdn.jsdelivr.net), extra Quran translations (api.alquran.cloud) and Quran recitation audio (cdn.islamic.network). These requests contain no personal information. "Mosques near me" opens your own maps app. Your tasbeeh counts, prayer tracker, zakat figures, bookmarks and settings stay on your device.

Sharing

When you choose to share an ayah, hadith or dua, the app opens your phone's share menu with the text (or a picture of it) and a link to the app on Google Play. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

PC / Windows version

On a computer (the Windows app or the website) everything described above is kept in the browser storage of that computer instead of the phone, and is never sent to us. Removing the app, or clearing its site data in the browser, deletes your name and all app data. Location is read only if you allow it in the browser, and the city name comes from the app's built-in city list. The PC version keeps the app and the texts you open in its offline cache so they also work without internet. It has no prayer reminders.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
