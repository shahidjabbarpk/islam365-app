Hajj Step by Step: What Happens on Each Day (8th to 13th Dhul Hijjah)

2026-11-10

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam, obligatory once in a lifetime for every adult Muslim who is able. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever performs Hajj and does not commit obscenity or wrongdoing returns like the day his mother gave birth to him" (Sahih Bukhari). This overview follows Hajj Tamattu', the type most pilgrims from South Asia perform: Umrah first, then Hajj.

Before the 8th: Umrah

Enter ihram for Umrah at the miqat, perform tawaf, sa'i and shorten or shave the hair, then leave ihram and wait in Makkah.

8th Dhul Hijjah: Yawm at-Tarwiyah

  • Enter ihram for Hajj from where you are staying and begin the talbiyah.
  • Go to Mina and pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and the next Fajr there.

9th: The Day of Arafah

  • After sunrise go to Arafah. Standing at Arafah is the heart of Hajj: "Hajj is Arafah" (Jami' at-Tirmidhi).
  • Spend the afternoon until sunset in dua, dhikr and Quran.
  • After sunset, go to Muzdalifah without praying Maghrib at Arafah. Pray Maghrib and Isha together in Muzdalifah, collect pebbles, and sleep under the open sky.

10th: Yawm an-Nahr (Eid al-Adha)

  1. After Fajr at Muzdalifah, go to Mina and stone the big Jamarah (Jamarat al-Aqabah) with seven pebbles, saying Allahu Akbar with each.
  2. Offer the sacrifice (qurbani).
  3. Shave or shorten the hair. Most ihram restrictions now end.
  4. Go to Makkah for Tawaf al-Ifadah and the sa'i of Hajj, then return to Mina.

In the Hanafi school, stoning, sacrifice and shaving should be done in this order.

11th, 12th (and 13th): Days of Tashreeq

  • Stay in Mina and each day after noon stone all three Jamarat: small, middle and big, seven pebbles each.
  • You may leave on the 12th before sunset, or stay and stone again on the 13th.

Farewell tawaf

Before leaving Makkah, perform Tawaf al-Wida' (not required for women in menses).
