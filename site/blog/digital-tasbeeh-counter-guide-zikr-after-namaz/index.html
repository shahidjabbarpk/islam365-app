Zikr, the remembrance of Allah, is one of the lightest acts of worship on the tongue and one of the heaviest on the scale. Allah says: "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest" (Quran 13:28). A tasbeeh counter simply helps you keep count, so your mind can stay on the meaning instead of the numbers.

Tasbeeh after every fard prayer

A well-known sunnah reported in Sahih Muslim is to say after each obligatory prayer:

  • SubhanAllah (Glory be to Allah) 33 times
  • Alhamdulillah (All praise is for Allah) 33 times
  • Allahu Akbar (Allah is the Greatest) 33 times
  • and to complete one hundred with: La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir

The hadith mentions that one's sins are forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea.

Tasbeeh-e-Fatima before sleep

When Sayyida Fatima (RA) asked for a servant, the Prophet ﷺ taught her and Ali (RA) something better: before sleeping say SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 34 times (Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim).

Why use a digital tasbeeh?

  • It is always in your pocket, in the masjid, on the bus or in the office.
  • You can set a target (33, 99, 100 or your own) and feel a vibration when you reach it.
  • Counting rounds is easy for larger numbers like 1,000 durood on Friday.
  • Seeing your daily total and streak motivates consistency. The most beloved deeds to Allah are the regular ones, even if they are small (Sahih Bukhari).
