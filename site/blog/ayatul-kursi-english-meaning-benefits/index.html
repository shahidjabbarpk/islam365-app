Ayatul Kursi, verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah, is the greatest verse of the Quran. The Prophet ﷺ asked Ubayy ibn Ka'b (RA) which verse was the greatest; he answered Ayatul Kursi, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "May knowledge be pleasant for you, Abu al-Mundhir" (Sahih Muslim).

Arabic

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَّهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَن ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

Transliteration

Allahu la ilaha illa huwal-hayyul-qayyum. La ta'khudhuhu sinatun wa la nawm. Lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ard. Man dhalladhi yashfa'u 'indahu illa bi-idhnih. Ya'lamu ma bayna aydihim wa ma khalfahum. Wa la yuhituna bi shay'im-min 'ilmihi illa bima sha'. Wasi'a kursiyyuhus-samawati wal-ard. Wa la ya'uduhu hifzuhuma. Wa huwal-'aliyyul-'azim.

Meaning

Allah: there is no god except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and preserving them does not tire Him. And He is the Most High, the Most Great.

Authentic benefits

  • Before sleeping: a guardian from Allah stays with you and no devil comes near until morning (Sahih Bukhari).
  • After every obligatory prayer: "nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death" (an-Nasa'i in al-Kubra, graded authentic).
  • It is part of the morning and evening adhkar.

Memorise it in 7 days

  1. Day 1-2: the first two sentences, up to wa la nawm.
  2. Day 3: lahu ma fis-samawati to illa bi-idhnih.
  3. Day 4-5: ya'lamu to illa bima sha'.
  4. Day 6: wasi'a kursiyyuhu to the end.
  5. Day 7: recite the whole verse after every prayer.
