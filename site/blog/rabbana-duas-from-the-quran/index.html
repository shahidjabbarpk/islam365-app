Many of the most powerful duas are in the Quran itself, often beginning with Rabbana ("Our Lord"). They are short, easy to memorise and perfect for sujood, after salah or any quiet moment.

1. Good in this world and the next

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. (Al-Baqarah 2:201). Anas (RA) said this was the dua the Prophet ﷺ made most often (Sahih Bukhari).

2. Acceptance of our deeds

Rabbana taqabbal minna innaka antas-sami'ul-'alim.

Our Lord, accept from us; You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. (2:127, the dua of Ibrahim and Isma'il while building the Kaaba)

3. Do not burden us

Rabbana la tu'akhidhna in nasina aw akhta'na.

Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake. (2:286)

4. Steadfast hearts

Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah, innaka antal-wahhab.

Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself; You are the Bestower. (Al Imran 3:8)

5. Forgiveness

Rabbana innana amanna faghfir lana dhunubana wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. (3:16)

6. The dua of Adam (AS)

Rabbana zalamna anfusana wa in lam taghfir lana wa tarhamna lanakunanna minal-khasirin.

Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers. (Al-A'raf 7:23)

7. Patience

Rabbana afrigh 'alayna sabran wa tawaffana muslimin.

Our Lord, pour patience upon us and let us die as Muslims. (7:126)

8. For salah and family

Rabbij-'alni muqimas-salati wa min dhurriyyati, rabbana wa taqabbal du'a.

My Lord, make me one who establishes prayer, and my descendants too. Our Lord, accept my dua. (Ibrahim 14:40)

9. For parents and all believers

Rabbanagh-fir li wa li walidayya wa lil-mu'minina yawma yaqumul-hisab.

Our Lord, forgive me, my parents and the believers on the Day the reckoning is established. (14:41)

10. Spouses and children

Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a'yunin waj'alna lil-muttaqina imama.

Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and children comfort to our eyes, and make us leaders of the righteous. (Al-Furqan 25:74)
