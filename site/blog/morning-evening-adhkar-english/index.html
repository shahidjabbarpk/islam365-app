Morning and Evening Adhkar in English: 6 Authentic Remembrances

Allah says: "Glorify Him morning and evening" (Quran 33:42). The morning adhkar are read between Fajr and sunrise, and the evening adhkar between Asr and Maghrib. They take about ten minutes and protect you all day.

1. Ayatul Kursi (once)

Whoever reads it at night is protected until morning (Sahih Bukhari). Read it in the morning and evening. Full text and meaning: Ayatul Kursi in English.

2. The three Quls (three times each)

Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas, three times each morning and evening: "they will suffice you against everything" (Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi).

3. Sayyidul Istighfar (once)

Allahumma anta Rabbi, la ilaha illa ant, khalaqtani wa ana 'abduk... Whoever says it with conviction and dies that day or night is of the people of Paradise (Sahih Bukhari). Full text: Sayyidul Istighfar.

4. Protection from all harm (three times)

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama'i wa huwas-sami'ul-'alim. In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in the heavens can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. Nothing will harm the one who says it three times (Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi).

5. Contentment (three times)

رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ نَبِيًّا

Raditu billahi Rabba, wa bil-Islami dina, wa bi Muhammadin ﷺ nabiyya. I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, Islam as my religion and Muhammad ﷺ as my Prophet. Allah promises to please the one who says it (Sunan Abu Dawud).

6. SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi (100 times)

No one will bring anything better on the Day of Judgement except someone who said the same or more (Sahih Muslim). Use a tasbeeh counter to keep count.
