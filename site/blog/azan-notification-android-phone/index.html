How to Get Azan and Prayer Time Notifications on Your Android Phone

2026-11-10

A phone that reminds you of every prayer is one of the simplest ways to stop missing salah. Here is how to set it up properly on Android, including the settings that make reminders arrive on time.

Step 1: Install a prayer times app

Install Islam 365 from Google Play. It is free, has no ads and does not need an account.

📲 Never forget it: get Islam 365 free for reminders before every prayer and these duas one tap away.

Step 2: Set your location

Open the app, allow location once (or pick your city manually). The app chooses the calculation method for your country automatically, for example Karachi with Hanafi Asr in Pakistan or Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia.

Step 3: Turn on the reminders you want

In More → Settings you can enable:

  • A reminder 10 to 30 minutes before each prayer (time for wudu)
  • A notification when the prayer time starts
  • A warning before the prayer time ends
  • A Friday reminder for Surah Al-Kahf

Allow notifications when Android asks.

Step 4: Allow "Alarms & reminders"

Android 12 and newer can delay ordinary notifications by up to an hour to save battery. Islam 365 asks for the Alarms & reminders permission so prayer reminders fire at the exact minute. Turn it on when the app shows the button.

Step 5: Stop battery saver from blocking reminders

  • All phones: Settings → Apps → Islam 365 → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Xiaomi / Redmi / Poco: also turn on Autostart for the app.
  • Oppo / Realme / Vivo: allow background activity and disable battery optimisation.
  • Samsung: remove the app from Sleeping apps in Device care → Battery.
  • Infinix / Tecno: allow auto-start in Phone Master.

Step 6: Open the app once a week

Reminders are scheduled several days ahead. Opening the app now and then refreshes them, and when you travel it updates the times for your new city automatically.

That is all. From today your phone will remind you before every namaz.
