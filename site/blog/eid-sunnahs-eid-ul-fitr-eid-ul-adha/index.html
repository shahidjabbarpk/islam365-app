Sunnahs of Eid: What to Do on Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha

The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah has given us two days of celebration: Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha (Sunan Abu Dawud). Eid is worship and joy together. These sunnahs help make it both.

Before the Eid prayer

  • Ghusl and wearing your best clothes. The companions bathed before going to the Eid prayer (Muwatta Malik).
  • Eid al-Fitr: eat before going, preferably an odd number of dates. The Prophet ﷺ would not go out on the day of Eid al-Fitr until he had eaten some dates, and he ate an odd number (Sahih Bukhari).
  • Eid al-Adha: delay eating until after the prayer, and eat from your qurbani if you have one (Jami' at-Tirmidhi).
  • Zakat al-Fitr must be given before the Eid al-Fitr prayer (Sahih Bukhari). Most people give it during the last days of Ramadan so it reaches the poor in time for Eid.

Takbeer

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, la ilaha illallah, wallahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, wa lillahil-hamd.

Say it on the way to the Eid prayer. For Eid al-Adha, the takbeer of tashreeq is said after every fard prayer from Fajr of 9 Dhul Hijjah to Asr of 13 Dhul Hijjah.

Going to the prayer

  • Go early, walking if possible.
  • Take a different route back. On the day of Eid the Prophet ﷺ would return by a different way (Sahih Bukhari).
  • Bring the whole family; the Prophet ﷺ even told women who did not pray to come and witness the good and the dua of the Muslims (Sahih Bukhari).

After the prayer

  • Greet each other: the companions would say Taqabbalallahu minna wa minkum, "May Allah accept from us and from you".
  • Visit family, give gifts and Eidi to children, and remember neighbours and the poor.
  • On Eid al-Adha, offer the qurbani after the Eid prayer, and share the meat with family, friends and those in need.
