Dua When It Rains, Dua for Strong Wind and After Rain

The Prophet ﷺ taught us to remember Allah at every change in the weather. Rain is a mercy, and a strong wind can bring good or harm, so these short duas ask for the good and seek protection from the harm.

1. When it rains

اللَّهُمَّ صَيِّبًا نَافِعًا

Allahumma sayyiban nafi'a. O Allah, (make it) a beneficial rain. The Prophet ﷺ said this when he saw rain (Sahih Bukhari).

2. After the rain

مُطِرْنَا بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ

Mutirna bi fadlillahi wa rahmatih. We have been given rain by the grace and mercy of Allah (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim). The same hadith warns against attributing rain to the stars.

3. When rain is too heavy

اللَّهُمَّ حَوَالَيْنَا وَلَا عَلَيْنَا

Allahumma hawalayna wa la 'alayna. O Allah, around us and not upon us. The Prophet ﷺ said this when heavy rain continued for a week in Madinah, and the clouds moved away (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim).

4. When a strong wind blows

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَهَا وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا وَخَيْرَ مَا أُرْسِلَتْ بِهِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا وَشَرِّ مَا أُرْسِلَتْ بِهِ

Allahumma inni as'aluka khayraha wa khayra ma fiha wa khayra ma ursilat bih, wa a'udhu bika min sharriha wa sharri ma fiha wa sharri ma ursilat bih. O Allah, I ask You for its good, the good within it and the good it was sent with, and I seek refuge in You from its evil, the evil within it and the evil it was sent with (Sahih Muslim).

Rain is a time for dua

A narration in Sunan Abu Dawud mentions the time of rainfall among the times when dua is not rejected. Use it to ask for your needs. See more times when dua is accepted.

Praying when it rains

When it rains heavily, the Prophet ﷺ allowed the call "pray in your homes" (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim). Rules on combining prayers because of rain differ between schools; Hanafi scholars do not allow combining for rain. Ask your local scholar about your situation.
