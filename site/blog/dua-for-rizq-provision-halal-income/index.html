Duas for Rizq: Asking Allah for Halal Provision and Barakah

Allah is Ar-Razzaq, the Provider. "There is no creature on earth except that its provision is due from Allah" (Quran 11:6). Effort is our duty; results are His. These duas help us ask in the right way.

1. The dua of Musa (AS)

رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ

Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqir. My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send down to me. (Quran 28:24). Musa (AS) said this as a stranger in Madyan with nothing; soon after, Allah gave him work, a home and a family.

2. After Fajr: knowledge, provision, accepted deeds

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbala. O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, pure provision and accepted deeds. The Prophet ﷺ said this after the Fajr salam (Sunan Ibn Majah).

3. Sufficiency with halal

اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ، وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ

Allahummak-fini bi halalika 'an haramik, wa aghnini bi fadlika 'amman siwak. O Allah, suffice me with what You have made lawful instead of what You have forbidden, and make me independent by Your grace of everyone besides You. Taught for relief from debt (Jami' at-Tirmidhi).

Habits that bring barakah in rizq

  • Istighfar: "Ask forgiveness of your Lord... He will provide you with wealth and children" (Quran 71:10-12).
  • Taqwa: "Whoever fears Allah, He will make a way out for him and provide for him from where he does not expect" (Quran 65:2-3).
  • Keeping family ties: whoever wants his provision expanded should keep ties of kinship (Sahih Bukhari).
  • Charity: wealth does not decrease because of charity (Sahih Muslim).
  • Starting the day early: "O Allah, bless my nation in their early mornings" (Sunan Abu Dawud).
