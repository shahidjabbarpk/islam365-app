Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, depending on the sighting of the moon in your country. That is only about three months away. The salaf used to prepare for Ramadan for months, so the month itself could be spent fully in worship. Here is a simple checklist you can start today.

3 months before: build the base

  • Fix your five prayers. Use a prayer tracker and aim for a full week with no missed prayer.
  • Start a daily Quran habit. Even one page a day. If you want to finish the Quran in Ramadan, that is about 20 pages a day, so start getting used to longer reading now.
  • Make up missed fasts from last year before the next Ramadan comes.

2 months before (Rajab): add voluntary fasts

  • Fast some Mondays and Thursdays, a sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, so your body gets used to fasting.
  • Learn the duas you will need: the dua for breaking the fast, and the Laylatul Qadr dua Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni.

1 month before (Sha'ban): plan the month

  • Calculate your Zakat so you can give it in Ramadan.
  • Plan your daily timetable: sehri, Fajr, work, Quran, iftar, Taraweeh, sleep.
  • Cut down on screen time and social media now, not on the first day of Ramadan.
