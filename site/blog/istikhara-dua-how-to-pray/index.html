Salat al-Istikhara: How to Pray and the Dua for Guidance

2026-11-09

Jabir (RA) said the Prophet ﷺ used to teach the companions Istikhara for all their matters, just as he taught them a surah of the Quran (Sahih Bukhari). Istikhara means asking Allah to choose what is best for you.

How to pray Istikhara

  1. Make wudu.
  2. Pray two rakat of voluntary prayer (not during the forbidden times, such as sunrise and right before Maghrib).
  3. After the prayer, praise Allah, send durood, and recite the Istikhara dua, naming your matter where the dua says "this matter".

The dua (transliteration)

Allahumma inni astakhiruka bi'ilmika, wa astaqdiruka bi qudratika, wa as'aluka min fadlikal-'azim. Fa innaka taqdiru wa la aqdir, wa ta'lamu wa la a'lam, wa anta 'allamul-ghuyub. Allahumma in kunta ta'lamu anna hadhal-amra khayrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa 'aqibati amri, faqdurhu li wa yassirhu li thumma barik li fih. Wa in kunta ta'lamu anna hadhal-amra sharrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa 'aqibati amri, fasrifhu 'anni wasrifni 'anhu, waqdur liyal-khayra haythu kana thumma ardini bih.

Meaning

O Allah, I seek Your guidance through Your knowledge, and I seek ability through Your power, and I ask You of Your great bounty. You have power and I have none, You know and I do not, and You are the Knower of the unseen. O Allah, if You know this matter is good for me in my religion, my life and my end, then decree it for me, make it easy for me and bless it for me. And if You know this matter is bad for me in my religion, my life and my end, then turn it away from me and turn me away from it, and decree for me what is good wherever it is, and make me pleased with it. (Sahih Bukhari)

Common misunderstandings

  • You do not need a dream. Nothing in the hadith says you will see a sign in your sleep. After Istikhara, go ahead with what seems right. If Allah makes it easy, that is the answer; if it gets blocked, that is also good for you.
  • You can pray it yourself. There is no need to pay someone to do Istikhara for you.
  • Consult people too. Istikhara is with Allah, and mashwara (advice) is with wise and trustworthy people. Do both.
  • You can repeat it if you are still unsure.
