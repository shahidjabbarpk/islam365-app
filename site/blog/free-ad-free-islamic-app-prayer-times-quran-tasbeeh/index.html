Islam 365: A Free, Ad-Free Islamic App for Prayer Times, Quran and Tasbeeh

All of this is in the Islam 365 app: prayer times, azan reminders, Quran and duas. Free, no ads.

Most of us keep three or four different apps for our daily deen: one for namaz timings, another for the Quran, a tasbeeh counter, and maybe a separate dua book. Many of them are full of ads that pop up right when you are trying to read an ayah. Islam 365 was built to fix that: one simple app that covers the everyday needs of a Muslim, and it is completely free with no ads.

What you get in Islam 365

🕌 Accurate prayer times wherever you are

Islam 365 calculates Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for your exact location. It also chooses the right calculation method for your country automatically: University of Islamic Sciences Karachi for Pakistan, Umm al-Qura for Saudi Arabia, ISNA for North America, Muslim World League, Dubai, Diyanet and others. Hanafi or Shafi'i Asr is picked for your country too, and you can change both in settings. When you travel to a new city, the times update for the new place and time zone on their own.

🔔 Reminders that actually help

  • A reminder 10 to 30 minutes before each prayer, so you have time for wudu
  • A notification when the prayer time starts
  • A warning before a prayer's time is about to end, so you never miss qaza
  • A Jumu'ah reminder for Surah Al-Kahf and durood
  • A new ayah, hadith or dua once a day at a random time

📖 The complete Quran, offline

Read the whole Quran in Uthmani script with Urdu and English translation built in. Indonesian, Turkish, Bengali, French and Russian translations can be added with one tap. You can listen to recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy ayah by ayah, search, bookmark, and continue from where you stopped. Browse by surah or by para.

📿 Digital tasbeeh counter

Tap anywhere on the screen to count, with a light vibration on each count. Set targets of 33, 99, 100 or your own number, keep rounds, and save your own zikr. The app keeps a record of today's count and your all-time total.

📚 Hadith, sunnat and duas

Read Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah and Muwatta in your language together with the Arabic. There is a hadith of the day, a list of everyday sunnahs with references, and morning and evening azkar plus duas for sleep, wudu, the masjid, food, travel and worry from Hisn al-Muslim.

🧭 Qibla, Zakat, Hijri calendar and more

A Qibla compass with the distance to Makkah, a Zakat calculator with gold or silver nisab, a "mosques near me" button, a Hijri calendar with Ramadan and Eid dates, sehri and iftar times in Ramadan, and the 99 Names of Allah with meanings.

📲 Never forget it: get Islam 365 free for reminders before every prayer and these duas one tap away.

Why no ads?

An Islamic app should help you focus on worship, not distract you from it. Islam 365 has no ads, no in-app purchases, and it does not ask you to sign up. Your name, prayer tracker and settings stay on your own phone.

Share khair with your family

Every ayah, hadith and dua in the app can be shared as text or as a beautiful picture for your WhatsApp status. The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever guides someone to good gets a reward like the one who does it (Sahih Muslim). If you find the app useful, share it with your family and friends.
