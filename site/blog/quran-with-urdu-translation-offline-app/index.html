Read the Quran with Urdu Translation Offline on Your Phone

2026-11-09

Many people in Pakistan and India want to understand what they recite, not only read the Arabic. A Quran app with a clear Urdu translation, that works without the internet, makes daily reading much easier, on the bus, at work or before sleeping.

What makes a good Quran app

  • Clear Arabic script. Islam 365 uses the Uthmani script with large, readable text.
  • Trusted translation. The Urdu translation is by Maulana Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry, one of the most widely read Urdu translations. English is built in too.
  • Works offline. The full Quran text and both translations are inside the app, so you can read with no data.
  • Recitation. Listen to Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy ayah by ayah, which also helps with correct pronunciation.
  • Easy navigation. Open by surah or by para (juz), search, bookmark ayahs, and continue where you left off.
  • More languages. Indonesian, Turkish, Bengali, French and Russian can be downloaded with one tap.

A simple daily plan

The Prophet ﷺ said that the most beloved deeds to Allah are those done regularly, even if small (Sahih Bukhari). Try this:

  1. After Fajr, read one page with its Urdu translation.
  2. Pick one ayah that touched you and share it with your family as a picture from the app.
  3. Before sleeping, listen to the same page once.

One page a day means you finish the Quran with understanding in about 20 months. Two pages a day, in under a year.
