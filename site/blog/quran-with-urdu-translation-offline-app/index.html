A simple daily plan

The Prophet ﷺ said that the most beloved deeds to Allah are those done regularly, even if small (Sahih Bukhari). Try this:

  1. After Fajr, read one page with its Urdu translation.
  2. Pick one ayah that touched you and share it with your family as a picture from the app.
  3. Before sleeping, listen to the same page once.

One page a day means you finish the Quran with understanding in about 20 months. Two pages a day, in under a year.
