Seeking knowledge is worship. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever takes a path seeking knowledge, Allah makes easy for him a path to Paradise" (Sahih Muslim). Here are duas every student can use before studying and before an exam.

1. For more knowledge

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Rabbi zidni 'ilma. My Lord, increase me in knowledge. (Quran 20:114). The only thing Allah told His Prophet ﷺ to ask for more of.

2. The dua of Musa (AS) for ease

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي

Rabbish-rahli sadri, wa yassir li amri, wahlul 'uqdatam-min lisani, yafqahu qawli. My Lord, expand my chest, make my task easy, and untie the knot from my tongue so they understand my speech. (Quran 20:25-28). Perfect before an exam, a presentation or an interview.

3. When something feels hard

اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا، وَأَنْتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ إِذَا شِئْتَ سَهْلًا

Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla, wa anta taj'alul-hazna idha shi'ta sahla. O Allah, nothing is easy except what You make easy, and You make the difficult easy if You wish. (Sahih Ibn Hibban)

4. For beneficial knowledge

Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'a. O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge (Sunan Ibn Majah). And seek refuge from knowledge that does not benefit (Sahih Muslim).

Study with barakah

  • Begin with Bismillah and a sincere intention to benefit yourself and others.
  • Study after Fajr when the mind is freshest.
  • Avoid sins and pray on time; Imam ash-Shafi'i said knowledge is a light, and Allah's light is not given to the disobedient.
  • Sleep well before the exam and say Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-wakil before you start.
