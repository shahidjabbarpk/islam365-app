How to Pray Salah Step by Step: A Beginner's Guide (Hanafi)

2026-11-09

Salah is the second pillar of Islam and the first thing we will be asked about on the Day of Judgement. If you are new to Islam, returning to prayer, or teaching your children, this guide walks through a two-rakat prayer according to the Hanafi school, followed by the rakat of every prayer.

Before you start

  • Be in a state of wudu, with clean body, clothes and place.
  • Cover the awrah (men: navel to knees; women: whole body except face, hands and feet).
  • Face the Qibla and make the intention in your heart for the prayer you are about to offer.

A two-rakat prayer, step by step

  1. Takbir: raise your hands to your ears (women to the shoulders) and say Allahu Akbar. Fold your hands, men below the navel, women on the chest.
  2. Thana: Subhanakallahumma wa bihamdika wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghayruk.
  3. Ta'awwudh and Bismillah quietly, then recite Surah Al-Fatiha, say Ameen quietly, and recite another surah or at least three short verses, for example Surah Al-Ikhlas.
  4. Ruku: say Allahu Akbar, bow with hands on knees and back straight, and say Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim three times.
  5. Stand up saying Sami'allahu liman hamidah, then Rabbana lakal-hamd.
  6. Sujood: say Allahu Akbar and prostrate on forehead, nose, palms, knees and toes. Say Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la three times.
  7. Sit briefly saying Allahu Akbar, then make a second sujood the same way. This completes the first rakat.
  8. Stand for the second rakat and repeat Al-Fatiha, a surah, ruku and two sujood.
  9. Sit for tashahhud: recite At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat... to the end. Then recite Durood Ibrahimi and a short dua such as Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah...
  10. Salam: turn your head right and say As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah, then left and say it again.

In a three- or four-rakat prayer, after the tashahhud in the second rakat, stand up for the remaining rakat (recite only Al-Fatiha in the extra fard rakat), and recite tashahhud, durood and dua in the final sitting.

Learning tips

  • Learn Al-Fatiha and three short surahs first; that is enough to pray.
  • Pray with a family member or at the masjid and follow along.
  • Use an app that tells you prayer times and reminds you, so the habit sticks.
