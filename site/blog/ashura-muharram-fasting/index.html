Muharram and Ashura: The Virtue of Fasting the 9th and 10th

Muharram is the first month of the Hijri year and one of the four sacred months (Quran 9:36). The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best fasting after Ramadan is in Allah's month of Muharram" (Sahih Muslim).

Why Ashura?

When the Prophet ﷺ came to Madinah, he found the Jews fasting on the 10th of Muharram. They said it was the day Allah saved Musa (AS) and his people from Pharaoh. The Prophet ﷺ said: "We are closer to Musa than you," and he fasted it and told the Muslims to fast (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim).

The reward

"Fasting the day of Ashura, I hope that Allah will expiate for the year before it" (Sahih Muslim).

Fast the 9th too

The Prophet ﷺ said: "If I live until next year, I will fast the 9th as well" (Sahih Muslim), to be different from the People of the Book. So it is sunnah to fast the 9th and 10th of Muharram. Some scholars also mention the 10th and 11th.

Other good deeds in Muharram

  • Extra voluntary fasts, especially Mondays and Thursdays.
  • Avoiding sins, which are more serious in the sacred months.
  • Starting the new Hijri year with a fresh intention and a new good habit.
