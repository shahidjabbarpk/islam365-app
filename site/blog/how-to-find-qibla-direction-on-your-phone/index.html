How to Find the Qibla Direction on Your Phone (Step by Step)

2026-11-09

Facing the Qibla, the Kaaba in Makkah, is a condition for salah. At home you usually know the direction, but in a hotel, an office, an airport or a new city it is easy to get confused. Your phone can show you the Qibla in a few seconds if you use it correctly.

How the Qibla direction is calculated

The Kaaba is at about 21.4225° N, 39.8262° E. A Qibla app takes your current location and works out the shortest path on the globe (the "great circle") from you to the Kaaba. The angle of that path from true north is your Qibla direction. From Pakistan and India the Qibla is roughly towards the west, a little south of west. From the UK it is roughly south-east, and from North America it is towards the north-east, not south-east as many people expect, because the Earth is round.

Finding Qibla with Islam 365

  1. Open Islam 365 and tap Qibla.
  2. Allow location once, or choose your city manually.
  3. Hold the phone flat, parallel to the floor, away from metal tables, magnets and speakers.
  4. Turn slowly until the Kaaba marker lines up with the top of the phone. The app also shows your distance to Makkah.

Calibrate your compass first

Phone compasses can drift. If the direction looks wrong or keeps jumping, move the phone in a figure-of-eight motion in the air a few times. This recalibrates the magnetic sensor. Also remove magnetic phone covers or car mounts while checking.

Common mistakes

  • Standing next to metal. Steel almirahs, cars, laptops and even reinforced concrete pillars can bend the reading by many degrees.
  • Holding the phone tilted. Keep it flat.
  • Wrong location. If you travelled, make sure the app has your new city.
  • Phone without a compass sensor. Some cheap phones have no magnetometer. In that case use the angle shown in the app together with the sun, or ask at a local masjid.

A small deviation is forgiven

Scholars explain that a person far from Makkah must face the general direction of the Kaaba (jihat al-Ka'bah), and a small error after making an honest effort does not invalidate the prayer. So do your best with the tools you have and pray with peace of mind.
