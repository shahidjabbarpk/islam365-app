Rajab is the seventh month of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar. In 1448 AH it is expected to begin on Thursday 10 December 2026 by the Umm al-Qura calendar, or a day later in countries that wait for local moon sighting such as Pakistan and India. Rajab is followed by Sha'ban and then Ramadan, which is expected around 8 February 2027.

One of the four sacred months

Allah says: "Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve... of these, four are sacred. That is the correct religion, so do not wrong yourselves during them" (Quran 9:36). The Prophet ﷺ named them in his farewell sermon: Dhul Qa'dah, Dhul Hijjah, Muharram, and Rajab of Mudar, which comes between Jumada and Sha'ban (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim).

Scholars explain that good deeds are more rewarding and sins are more serious in these months. So Rajab is a good time to stop a bad habit and build a good one before Ramadan arrives.

What you can do in Rajab

  • Voluntary fasts: the general Sunnah fasts such as Monday and Thursday and the three white days (13, 14, 15 of the Hijri month) apply in Rajab as in any other month. See Monday, Thursday and white-day fasting.
  • Make up missed fasts: if you owe fasts from last Ramadan, the coming two months are the time to complete them.
  • Start a Quran routine: a few pages a day from now means you reach Ramadan already in the habit.
  • More istighfar and charity: see the virtue of istighfar.

The well-known Rajab dua

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَنَا فِي رَجَبٍ وَشَعْبَانَ وَبَلِّغْنَا رَمَضَانَ

Allahumma barik lana fi Rajaba wa Sha'bana wa ballighna Ramadan. O Allah, bless us in Rajab and Sha'ban and let us reach Ramadan. This is reported in Musnad Ahmad, but hadith scholars graded its chain weak. Many scholars say there is no harm in making this as a general dua without believing it is a confirmed Sunnah.

What is not established

There is no authentic hadith that sets a special prayer, a specific number of fasts, or a special reward only for Rajab. Prayers such as "Salat ar-Raghaib" on the first Thursday night of Rajab are not found in the authentic collections. Keep to the general Sunnah acts above. For questions about practices in your community, ask a trusted local scholar.

Important nights coming up

  • 27 Rajab (around 4–5 January 2027): commonly observed as Shab-e-Meraj in South Asia.
  • 15 Sha'ban (around 22–23 January 2027): Shab-e-Barat.
  • 1 Ramadan (around 8 February 2027): see the Ramadan 2027 timetable.
