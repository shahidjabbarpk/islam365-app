How to Perform Umrah: A Simple Step-by-Step Guide

Umrah is called the "lesser pilgrimage". It can be done at any time of the year and takes only a few hours once you are in Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "One Umrah to the next is an expiation for what is between them" (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim). Here are the four main steps.

Step 1: Ihram

  • Before reaching the miqat (for flights from Pakistan and India, usually announced in the plane before Jeddah), take a bath, and men wear the two white unstitched sheets. Women wear normal modest clothes.
  • Pray two rakat if it is not a forbidden time, make the intention for Umrah, and begin the talbiyah:

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Labbayk Allahumma labbayk, labbayka la sharika laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak. (Sahih Bukhari)

In ihram avoid: cutting hair or nails, perfume, hunting, marital relations, and for men covering the head and wearing stitched clothes.

Step 2: Tawaf

  • Stop the talbiyah when you start tawaf. Begin at the line of the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad); kiss or touch it if possible, otherwise point towards it and say Bismillahi Allahu Akbar.
  • Go around the Kaaba seven times anticlockwise, keeping it on your left. Men uncover the right shoulder (idtiba') during this tawaf, and walk briskly in the first three rounds (raml) if possible.
  • Between the Yemeni corner and the Black Stone recite: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar (Sunan Abu Dawud).
  • After tawaf, pray two rakat behind Maqam Ibrahim if possible, or anywhere in the mosque, and drink Zamzam.

Step 3: Sa'i

  • Go to Safa, face the Kaaba, praise Allah and make dua.
  • Walk to Marwa: that is one round. Marwa back to Safa is the second. Complete seven rounds, ending at Marwa.
  • Men jog lightly between the two green lights.

Step 4: Halq or taqsir

Men shave the head (halq, better) or cut the hair short all over (taqsir). Women cut about a fingertip's length from the end of their hair. Your Umrah is now complete and the restrictions of ihram end.

Useful tips

  • Go for tawaf late at night or after Fajr when it is less crowded.
  • Keep your shoes in a bag with you; it is easy to lose them.
  • Make a list of duas for your family before you travel.
