99 Names of Allah (Asma ul Husna): Meaning and How to Learn Them

2026-11-09

Allah says: "And to Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them" (Quran 7:180). The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise" (Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim). Scholars explain that "enumerating" means learning them, understanding them and living by their meaning.

A few of the names and their meanings

Al-QayyumالْقَيُّومُThe Self-Sustaining

How to learn them in one month

  1. Learn 3 or 4 names a day. In about 30 days you will have all 99.
  2. Read the meaning and think of one way the name shows in your life. When you eat, remember Ar-Razzaq.
  3. Use the names in your dua: "Ya Ghaffar, forgive me", "Ya Razzaq, grant me halal rizq".
  4. Revise the previous days' names before starting new ones.
