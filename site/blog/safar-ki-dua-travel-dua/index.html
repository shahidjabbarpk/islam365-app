Dua for Travelling (Safar ki Dua) and Sunnahs of a Journey

Whether it is a bus ride to another city, a flight for work or a journey for Umrah, the Prophet ﷺ taught us to begin every journey with remembrance of Allah.

The dua when you sit in your transport

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ

Subhanalladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila rabbina lamunqalibun.

"Glory be to the One who has subjected this to us, and we could never have done it ourselves. And surely to our Lord we will return." (Quran 43:13-14). The Prophet ﷺ would say Allahu Akbar three times and then recite this when setting out on a journey (Sahih Muslim).

Sunnahs of travel

  • Shortening prayers (qasr): a traveller prays two rakat instead of four for Dhuhr, Asr and Isha. Fajr and Maghrib stay the same. Scholars differ on the distance and the number of days; in the Hanafi school it applies to a journey of about 78 km or more and a stay of less than 15 days.
  • Combining prayers: allowed in the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools when travelling; the Hanafi school does not allow real combining except at Arafah and Muzdalifah. Follow your madhab.
  • Travelling in company and choosing a leader among the group.
  • Making dua: the dua of a traveller is accepted (Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi).
