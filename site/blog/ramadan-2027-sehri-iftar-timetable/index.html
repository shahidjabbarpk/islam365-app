How long will the fasts be?

Because Ramadan 2027 falls in February and March, the days are short in South Asia and the Gulf: roughly 12 to 13 hours of fasting, growing by a few minutes each day.

Sehri: don't stop too early, don't eat too late

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged delaying sehri until shortly before Fajr (Sahih Bukhari). Many printed calendars stop sehri a few minutes before Fajr as a precaution. Whatever you follow, stop eating before the Fajr time.

Iftar: hurry when the sun sets

"People will remain upon goodness as long as they hasten to break the fast" (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim). Break it with dates or water and make dua: the dua for breaking the fast.
