The few minutes after salah are precious. Instead of getting up straight away, these sunnah adhkar take about five minutes.

1. Astaghfirullah (3 times)

The Prophet ﷺ would seek forgiveness three times after finishing the prayer (Sahih Muslim).

2. Allahumma antas-salam

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ

Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam, tabarakta ya dhal-jalali wal-ikram. O Allah, You are Peace and from You is peace. Blessed are You, O Owner of majesty and honour (Sahih Muslim).

3. Ayatul Kursi

"Whoever recites Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory prayer, nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death" (an-Nasa'i, al-Kubra).

4. Tasbeeh: 33 + 33 + 33 + 1

SubhanAllah 33, Alhamdulillah 33, Allahu Akbar 33, then La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir. His sins are forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea (Sahih Muslim).

5. The dua taught to Mu'adh (RA)

اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَىٰ ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ

Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik. O Allah, help me to remember You, thank You and worship You well. The Prophet ﷺ told Mu'adh not to leave it after any prayer (Sunan Abu Dawud, an-Nasa'i).
