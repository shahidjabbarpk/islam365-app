The Four Stories of Surah Al-Kahf and Their Lessons for Today

Reading Surah Al-Kahf on Friday brings light between the two Fridays (al-Hakim, al-Bayhaqi). The Prophet ﷺ also said whoever memorises the first ten verses of Surah Al-Kahf is protected from the Dajjal (Sahih Muslim). The surah is built around four stories, and each one is about a trial every person faces.

1. The people of the cave: the trial of faith (verses 9-26)

A group of young believers lived under a ruler who persecuted them for their faith. They left everything, took shelter in a cave, and Allah made them sleep for 309 years. When they woke, their town had become believers.

Lesson: protect your faith even when it costs you. Good company matters; they were strong because they were together.

2. The owner of two gardens: the trial of wealth (verses 32-44)

A man was given two rich gardens. He became proud, said "I don't think this will ever perish", and forgot Allah. His friend advised him to say "Masha'Allah, la quwwata illa billah". In the end his gardens were destroyed.

Lesson: everything we own is from Allah and can be taken back. Say Masha'Allah when you see something you like.

3. Musa and Khidr: the trial of knowledge (verses 60-82)

Musa (AS) travelled to learn from Khidr, a servant of Allah given special knowledge. Khidr damaged a boat, killed a boy and repaired a wall for people who refused them food. Each act looked wrong, but each had hidden wisdom.

Lesson: however much we know, Allah knows more. When things happen that we do not understand, trust that Allah's plan has wisdom.

4. Dhul-Qarnayn: the trial of power (verses 83-98)

A just king who travelled east and west was given great power. He used it to help the weak, building a barrier to protect a people from Ya'juj and Ma'juj, and said: "This is a mercy from my Lord."

Lesson: power and position are a trust. Use them to serve people, and give credit to Allah.
