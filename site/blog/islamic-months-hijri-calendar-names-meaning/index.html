The 12 Islamic Months: Names, Meanings and Important Dates in the Hijri Calendar

2026-11-09

The Islamic (Hijri) calendar is a lunar calendar of 12 months. Each month starts with the sighting of the new crescent and has 29 or 30 days, so the Hijri year is about 11 days shorter than the solar year. That is why Ramadan moves earlier every year. The calendar counts from the Hijrah of the Prophet ﷺ from Makkah to Madinah.

The four sacred months

Allah says: "Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve... of these, four are sacred" (Quran 9:36). These are Muharram, Rajab, Dhul Qa'dah and Dhul Hijjah. Good deeds are encouraged and wrongdoing is more serious in them.

Where we are now

Today we are in Jumada al-Ula 1448 AH. Ramadan 1448 is expected around early February 2027, depending on the moon.
